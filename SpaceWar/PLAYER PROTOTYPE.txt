PLAYER GAMEPLAY :
# SPACEWAR — PLAYER SYSTEM DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION

## 1. Player Overview

The player controls a spaceship travelling through hostile space.

The Player system is responsible for:
- Movement and rotation.
- Physics and acceleration.
- Player input.
- Health and damage.
- Shooting.
- Dash / boost.
- Healing collection.
- Player-related visual feedback.

**Core principle:** Gameplay must be implemented and tested using placeholder graphics before introducing final sprites, animations, and visual effects.

---

## 2. Player Controls

### Confirmed controls

| Action | Input |
|---|---|
| Rotation | Mouse |
| Forward thrust | W (recommended binding) |
| Reverse thrust | S (recommended binding) |
| Continuous shooting | Toggle using left mouse button (recommended binding) |
| Dash / boost | Space |
| Pause | Deferred to menu/UI development |

The W and S bindings are recommended defaults rather than explicitly finalized key assignments.

### Rotation

- The spaceship continuously faces the mouse cursor.
- Rotation is instantaneous toward the cursor.
- The mouse controls the direction of aiming and thrust.

### Pause

Pause functionality and its interface will be addressed during menu/UI development, not the initial Player implementation.

---

## 3. Movement Physics

**Status: CONFIRMED**

SPACEWAR uses physics-based movement.

### Movement behavior

- The spaceship accelerates in the direction it faces.
- Forward thrust activates through keyboard input.
- Reverse thrust applies acceleration in the opposite direction.
- Forward and reverse thrust have equal strength.
- Releasing thrust does not stop the spaceship.
- The spaceship gradually loses speed through natural drag.
- Maximum normal speed is fixed.

### Physics parameters

| Parameter | Decision |
|---|---|
| Acceleration | Moderate |
| Maximum speed | Fixed |
| Natural drag | Slow |
| Reverse thrust | Same strength as forward |
| Rotation | Instant toward mouse |
| Movement calculation | Velocity and acceleration |
| Time calculation | Delta time |

Exact numerical values will be tuned through testing.

### Thruster effects

- Exhaust appears behind the spaceship while thrust is active.
- Exhaust stops when thrust is released.
- Initial implementation uses simple placeholder shapes.

---

## 4. Dash / Boost

**Status: CONFIRMED**

- Activated manually using Space.
- Boosts the spaceship in its current facing direction.
- Temporarily exceeds normal maximum speed.
- Lasts a few seconds.
- Has a cooldown.
- Can be activated while already at maximum normal speed.

Exact duration, boost strength, and cooldown values will be determined through testing.

---

## 5. Camera and Environment

**Status: CONFIRMED**

### Camera

- Smoothly follows the spaceship.
- Keeps the spaceship at the center of the screen.
- Uses fixed zoom.
- Does not rotate with the spaceship.

### Environment

- Each level has defined boundaries.
- Boundaries are invisible.
- The player cannot leave the playable area.
- Boundary collision should prevent the spaceship from escaping the level.

---

## 6. Health and Survival

**Status: CONFIRMED / INITIAL BALANCING VALUES**

### Health

- Starting health: 100%.
- Maximum health: 100%.
- Health displayed through a health bar.

### Lives

- Initial number of lives: 3.
- Lives are shared across the five-level campaign.
- Death restarts the current level.
- Previously completed levels remain completed.

### Damage values

| Damage source | Damage |
|---|---:|
| Level 1 enemy projectile | 2% |
| Level 2 enemy projectile | 5% |
| Level 3 enemy projectile | 8% |
| Enemy contact | 10% |
| Asteroid collision | 10% |
| Boss attack | 10% |

These are initial balancing values and may be adjusted after gameplay testing.

### Temporary invulnerability

**Confirmed duration: 1 second.**

After receiving damage:
- The player becomes temporarily invulnerable.
- Additional damage is ignored during this period.
- The purpose is to prevent overlapping collisions and projectiles from instantly draining the health bar.

### Critical health

When health reaches 10% or less:
- A heartbeat-like screen effect begins.
- Screen darkening remains subtle.
- The spaceship flashes red relatively quickly.
- Future audio effects will reinforce the warning.

### Death sequence

1. Health reaches zero.
2. One life is deducted immediately.
3. Player movement, rotation, and shooting are disabled.
4. A black hole appears.
5. The black hole consumes the spaceship.
6. The current level restarts.
7. The spaceship emerges from a black hole as the level begins.

The animation should be brief but clear.

## 7. Healing Stones

**Status: CONFIRMED**

| Property | Decision |
|---|---|
| Healing per stone | 1% |
| Disappearance time | 3 seconds |
| Spawn positions | Random |
| Spawn frequency | Scaled according to player health |
| Collection at full health | Allowed, without increasing health |

### Spawn behavior

Healing stones should appear randomly.

Their spawn frequency should increase when the player's health is low.

This creates additional opportunities for recovery when the player is struggling.

The exact health thresholds, spawn probabilities, and minimum/maximum spawn intervals remain to be determined.

### Collection

- A stone disappears after collection.
- It restores 1% health.
- A stone disappears automatically after three seconds if not collected.
- Collecting a stone at full health does not increase health above 100%.

---

## 8. Shooting System

**Status: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**

### Starting weapon

The player starts with a basic shooter.

### Shooting activation

**Toggle mode:**
- Press the shooting button once to activate continuous shooting.
- Press it again to deactivate shooting.

The left mouse button is the recommended binding.

### Projectile behavior

- One projectile is fired per shot.
- Continuous shooting can produce multiple successive projectiles.
- Projectiles travel in the direction the spaceship faces.
- Projectile speed is fixed.
- A projectile disappears after hitting an enemy.
- A projectile that misses disappears after reaching its maximum lifetime or travel distance.

### Firing frequency

Medium firing frequency.

The exact firing interval remains to be tuned.

Projectile creation and behavior belong to the Bullet system, not exclusively to Player.

---

## 9. Future Player Abilities

### Shooting Stars

A future special attack:
- Can destroy enemies.
- Can damage bosses.
- Will not be available in the earliest levels.

### Black Hole Portal

A future ability planned for a later or final level:
- Transports the player to another position in the environment.
- Can be used to evade enemy attacks.
- Allows the player to attack from another position.

This ability is separate from the black hole death animation.
